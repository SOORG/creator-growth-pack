# Outreach frameworks — examples, not validated performance claims

## Subjects

- a question about your {product}
- partnership idea for {niche}
- quick question about your creator storefront

Never claim "made this for you" unless you actually made it. Avoid fabricated time-to-set-up claims.

## Variants

**Existing audience:** Refer to one real, supplied public work sample; explain fit and the verified platform benefit. Ask permission to share details.

**Emerging creator:** Mention the creator's actual format and the verified onboarding options. Do not assume audience size, cost, payout speed or eligibility.

**New creator:** Focus on learning what they make. Avoid promising free services, hosted checkout or operational features not confirmed.

## Suggested sequence

Day 1: specific relevance → verified offer → optional question → opt-out.

Day 4: one useful concrete detail (not fictional proof) → low-pressure reply option → opt-out.

Day 9: a respectful final check-in → close the thread after silence or decline.

## Measure without inventing outcomes

Track only consentfully collected, aggregated counts: sent, delivered, responses, positive replies and opt-outs. Define denominators before calculating rates. No default performance targets or benchmarks are claimed.
