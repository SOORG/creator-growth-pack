# Creator Outreach

## Scope

Write **drafts**, never send emails, scrape contacts, schedule outreach or claim consent/compliance. Ask for the audience, creator niche, verified public personalization, offer terms and allowed channel. Request missing essentials in no more than two concise questions; otherwise draft with `[VERIFY]` markers.

## Workflow

1. Identify the purpose, intended recipient, jurisdiction/channel and verified platform terms (fee, payout, availability, reward). Never substitute pack examples for actual operator terms.
2. Use only a creator's *provided or checkable public* work for personalization; otherwise open with neutral relevance, not a made-up observation.
3. Produce three short, distinct messages: initial contact (Day 1), one relevant follow-up (Day 4), respectful close (Day 9). These are suggested intervals, not an instruction to send automatically.
4. Each message: subject, 70–120 words maximum, one relevant benefit with evidence, one low-friction question, genuine sender identification and a simple opt-out option appropriate to the medium.
5. Include an optional one-line final close only if local rules and recipient preference permit. Stop immediately after a decline, unsubscribe or other opt-out.
6. Finish with a **Verification before sending** section listing all unsupported claims, eligibility issues and unapproved terms.

## Quality rules

- No invented creator posts, endorsements, follower counts, earnings, testimonials, urgency, scarcity or response-rate claims.
- Numbers only when supported and relevant; otherwise use `[VERIFY: approved offer]`. Never guarantee income or conversion.
- Avoid deceptive subjects, spam, purchased lists and bulk outreach instructions. Respect privacy and applicable direct-marketing laws and platform policies.
- If the user has not approved sending, remain in DRAFT status.
