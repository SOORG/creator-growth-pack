# Product Listing Copy

## Input and output

Ask for product type, intended buyer, concrete deliverables/files, supported formats/compatibility, permitted usage, marketplace/currency and verified price constraints. If essential details are missing, write a **DRAFT** using `[VERIFY]` markers and list required confirmations. Do not infer the contents of a digital download from an illustrative template.

Provide, where supported by the input:

1. Keyword-led title (aim for at most 80 characters; platform rules take precedence).
2. Concise hook (aim for at most 120 characters).
3. Benefit-first description (150–300 words when facts permit; do not pad with guesses).
4. Five to seven feature bullets supported by actual capabilities.
5. Up to fifteen specific tags without irrelevant names or misleading SEO promises.
6. Price range with explicit currency, assumptions and comparison method; never override platform minimums or current verified fee/refund rules.
7. **Exactly what's included**, using a confirmed file list. Unknown files must be marked `[VERIFY: filenames/formats]`, never invented.
8. Buyer-fit and limitations: requirements, compatibility, support, usage rights and disclosure of material restrictions.

## Accuracy gate

- Do not fabricate file counts, screenshots, testimonials, product compatibility, licenses or outcomes.
- Do not assert a third-party commercial-use license unless the seller supplied and confirmed its exact terms.
- No guaranteed sales, unverifiable superlatives or competitor impersonation.
- Draft only: no automatic listing creation, publication, price changes or customer communications.

Read `references/listing-formulas.md` for **illustrative** formulas and examples.
