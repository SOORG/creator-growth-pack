# Hook ideas — safe structures, not factual claims

Replace every `{...}` field only with real, confirmed information. Numbers, customer quotes, before/after results and promotional terms require evidence.

## Final check

Do not turn illustrative hooks into unsupported claims. Verify offer availability, statistics, money saved, customer quotes, feature status and attribution before publishing.
