# Social Content Engine

## Workflow

1. Get product and confirmed benefits, audience, platform(s), actual posting capacity, objective, proof available, any approved offer and campaign start. Ask for missing essentials briefly or mark `[VERIFY]`.
2. Produce a 30-day table with Day, Channel, Publish/Rest, Format, Hook, Angle, CTA and Evidence/approval needed. **Only schedule as many posts as the user can produce**; rest days must be labelled clearly.
3. Across *planned posts*, aim for a balanced mix of teaching, behind-the-scenes or verified proof, offers and creator story. An illustrative starting mix is 40/30/20/10; do not enforce precise percentages on small samples.
4. Draft up to seven scheduled posts in full, including hook, caption, accessibility note and optional relevant hashtags. Do not invent activity on rest days.
5. Keep hook lines concise (approximately 12 words or fewer) and adapted to the channel; format, length and hashtag count depend on the platform.
6. Return a publication checklist and an explicit **DRAFT, not posted or scheduled** status.

## Guardrails

Never fabricate usage metrics, scarcity, savings, testimonials, giveaways, discounts, free offers or platform tests. Do not imply a post was scheduled, published or monitored. Treat numeric claims and testimonials from the hooks reference as **placeholders requiring proof**. Review affiliate/sponsorship disclosure, copyright, privacy and each channel's policies before publication.

See `references/hooks-library.md` for safe hook structures.
