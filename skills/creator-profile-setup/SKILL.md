# Creator Profile Setup

## Workflow

1. Gather creator handle/name, niche, target buyer, actual products, target platform and preferred first-person or third-person voice. Ask briefly for missing essentials; mark remaining unknowns `[VERIFY]`.
2. Propose up to three display name/handle ideas (availability **not checked** unless an authorized tool verified it).
3. Write a concise bio in the creator's requested voice, prioritizing factual product focus, buyer relevance and one CTA. Aim for 150 characters if no platform limit was supplied, and report the character count.
4. Produce avatar and banner **briefs**, not actual uploaded media. Recommend legible/mobile-safe visuals; adjust dimensions to confirmed platform specifications.
5. Propose a product shelf order only from real supplied products and verified sales data. Do not invent best sellers or require an arbitrary number of products.
6. Provide the readiness checklist from `assets/profile-checklist.md`, marking optional items as optional and pending items as blocked/unverified.
7. End with **Draft / Ready for human review / Blocked**. Never represent a profile as activated or live without real verification.

## Safeguards

Do not invent followers, client names, awards, sales, testimonials or endorsements. Do not collect banking IDs, identity documents or tax numbers in chat; direct creators to verified secure onboarding channels. Payment/KYC, tax, identity, license and delivery checks are determined by the operator's current policy, not by this skill.
